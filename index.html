    WHAT IS SOLAR ENERGY?

    +

    Solar energy is energy from the sun converted into electricity using solar panels. It's a clean, renewable source that reduces electricity bills and environmental impact.

    WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF USING SOLAR PANELS?

    +

    Solar panels help reduce electricity bills, are eco-friendly, and increase property value. They also provide energy independence and qualify for tax incentives.

    HOW LONG DO SOLAR PANELS LAST?

    +

    Most solar panels last between 25–30 years with proper maintenance. They typically come with performance warranties guaranteeing 80% output after 25 years.

    WHAT MAINTENANCE DO SOLAR PANELS REQUIRE?

    +

    Solar panels require simple cleaning and occasional inspection. Rain naturally washes away dust, but professional cleaning once a year is recommended for optimal performance.
